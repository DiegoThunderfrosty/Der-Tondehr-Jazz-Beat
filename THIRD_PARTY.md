## iPlug2

iPlug2 provides the plugin, standalone and user-interface framework. The
compatible revision is pinned in `scripts/setup_dependencies.ps1`. Its
zlib-style notice is preserved in `docs/licenses/iPlug2-LICENSE.txt` and in the
downloaded checkout.

## VST3 SDK

The VST3 SDK provides the plugin-format interfaces. The pinned source reports
version 3.8.1 and is distributed under the MIT License. Its notice is preserved
in `docs/licenses/VST3-LICENSE.txt` and in the downloaded checkout.

## Transitive components

iPlug2 includes or uses components such as HIIR, WDL, RtAudio, RtMidi, NanoVG
and NanoSVG. Their notices remain in the iPlug2 checkout. Binary distributors
must retain every notice required by linked components.

This repository does not distribute third-party manuals, circuit drawings,
recordings, sessions, impulse responses or downloaded dependency checkouts.
